About this tool

Built for field use by hydrogeologists, drillers, technical officers and engineers carrying out Vertical Electrical Sounding (VES) surveys. It records AB/2 and apparent resistivity readings, plots the standard log–log sounding curve, flags possible reading errors, and produces a printable field report. It does not auto-interpret layer resistivity, thickness or aquifer potential — that judgement stays with the qualified hydrogeologist reviewing the curve.

Works fully offline. All data stays in this browser's local storage on this device — export a backup regularly, especially before clearing browser data or switching devices.
